How to play

1. Forage for food. Workers also collect food while the game is running. Soldiers and workers consume a little food each second.

2. Hatch workers for income. Dig a nursery when you hit your worker limit.

3. To expand underground, select Shovel, dig a brown square next to an existing room or tunnel, then select a room and click an empty tunnel.

4. Rooms do different jobs: nurseries fit more workers, granaries hold more food, barracks allow more soldiers, fungus farms generate food, and guard posts improve defense.

5. Threat rises as the colony becomes more active. When a raid is announced, train soldiers, move workers to guard duty, or pay for an emergency barricade.

6. An attack can steal food, kill ants, and hurt the queen. If her health reaches zero, you lose. Heal her in the shop before that happens.

Goal: survive 3 raids, build 8 rooms (including the queen chamber), and train 6 soldiers.

Ant facts

Most worker ants are female. Ant colonies can have specialized jobs such as foraging, brood care, and colony defense. Ants use chemical signals called pheromones to communicate.

Real ant species vary widely. This is a made-up strategy game, not an accurate biology simulator.
